\footnotesize
$^{a}$ WiLoR + SLAM on detbox v3: per-frame WiLoR cropped from our detbox v3, composed with the same
DROID-SLAM trajectory as our HaWoR run. WiLoR is \emph{weak-perspective} (dummy focal), so its absolute
placement is not metric: composed C-MPJPE and W-MPJPE are $\sim$1\,m regardless of resolution (verified on
native-HD, focal-corrected: GT-box C-MPJPE 1152; the pose itself is good, root-relative 51\,mm) - the
documented weak-perspective failure. Hence only the alignment-invariant WA-MPJPE (33.4) is metric-meaningful.
Vanilla HaMeR + SLAM behaves identically (weak-perspective); the metric HaMeR + SLAM rows are the
absolute-depth-tuned HaMeR$_{\text{mt/ft}}$ above.
$^{b}$ HaWoR on detbox v3: the full pipeline (DROID-SLAM + Metric3D + motion + infiller) run
end-to-end on all 157 sequences with our boxes injected in place of its own detector, 0 sequence
failures. On its \emph{own} tuned detector it scores 49.7 / 34.7 / 42.3; on our boxes it degrades to
87.7 / 40.1 / 53.4. This is the fair, input-matched number: our detbox v3 is genuinely noisier than
HaWoR's detector (recall $\sim$0.78, IoU-vs-GT $\sim$0.5), and HaWoR's motion+infiller is tuned to
its own box statistics, so under a common realistic detector our jitter-robust model holds up while
HaWoR does not.
$^{c}$ HaPTIC + SLAM on detbox v3: HaPTIC is weak-perspective, so its metric depth is not
recoverable. We verified this deeply on the 157-seq run - absolute C-MPJPE is 157.7 with a
\emph{depth-dependent} error (C-abs/C-rr ratio median 4.4, spread 2.4-8.0), while root-relative
C-MPJPE is a healthy 29.2: the pose is good, only metric placement fails (the classic weak-perspective
mode, amplified at the 224px crop focal). HaPTIC does no camera tracking, so for a fair world-space
number we compose its per-frame camera-space hand with the \emph{same} DROID-SLAM trajectory as the
WiLoR/HaMeR+SLAM rows (shared HaWoR trajectory, recovered SE(3) residual 0.000\,mm), then score
identically. Only the alignment-invariant WA-MPJPE (35.7) is metric-meaningful; C-MPJPE and W-MPJPE
are omitted as weak-perspective.
$^{d}$ Hand3R: reported paper numbers; it cannot be re-run on our boxes without standing up its
pipeline, so it is a reference row (not input-matched). C-MPJPE is absolute camera-frame, matching
our C-MPJPE.
$^{e}$ All of our numbers come from a single end-to-end feedforward run (our detbox v3, no SLAM,
predicted extrinsics, 30-frame WA window); the head is trained jitter-robust, so it tolerates the
same noisy detboxes that degrade the offline baselines. Feedforward FPS = 6.59, measured on an NVIDIA
GB10 (mean over 30 timed clips: 2427.5\,ms per 16-frame forward+lift, the clip length the eval that
produces our C/WA/W numbers actually runs; a single 30-frame forward pass is 4.5\,fps). Total params
1178.7\,M, trainable 262.4\,M.
$^{f}$ Offline FPS is MEASURED, not estimated: the full HaWoR pipeline (detect + DROID-SLAM +
Metric3D + motion + infiller) ran 157 sequences of 300-frame video in 5\,h\,18\,min wall-clock on a
datacenter GPU = 2.47 native fps, dominated by offline DROID-SLAM (global bundle adjustment). The
per-frame hand regressor alone is 6.9\,fps (113.4\,min for 157 seqs $\times$ 300 frames); SLAM is the
bottleneck the three offline rows share. The load-bearing distinction is \emph{online/feedforward}
(single causal pass, no future frames, no global optimization) vs \emph{offline} (whole-video SLAM),
not raw throughput on matched hardware.
$^{g}$ HaMeR$_{\text{mt}}$/HaMeR$_{\text{ft}}$: HaMeR fine-tuned on HOI4D with our absolute-depth (kp3d\_abs)
recipe (mt = metric-tuned translation head; ft = our stronger fine-tune), per-frame on detbox v3, composed
with the same DROID-SLAM trajectory. These show our recipe transfers to a HaMeR backbone (fine-tuned
HaMeR$_{\text{ft}}$+SLAM reaches C-MPJPE 37.4, on par with our feedforward 36.2) - but they remain
\emph{offline} (need whole-video SLAM), whereas ours is a single online pass. Composed SE(3) residual 0.003\,mm.
$^{h}$ Dyn-HaMR input-matched: its per-frame hand estimator seeded from our detbox v3 (WiLoR init) and
its DROID-SLAM stage replaced by our SLAM cameras, so the same boxes + trajectory feed it as every other
row; PHALP tracking and DROID are bypassed. Dyn-HaMR is an \emph{optimization} method (per-sequence
test-time fitting with a motion prior); on ego HOI4D it is unstable -- the optimizer drives many
sequences to a degenerate world-scale / hand-shape (inflated MANO betas, occasional negative scale),
so its \emph{absolute} C-MPJPE and global W-MPJPE blow up (972 / 139\,mm) and are omitted as
unreliable; only the alignment-invariant WA-MPJPE (48.3) is meaningful. This is not Dyn-HaMR's native
regime (it is built for its own PHALP + DROID pipeline). Current subset: 27 sequences (25 converged;
extending to 157). Runs on the same offline SLAM trajectory (FPS 2.47$^{f}$).
