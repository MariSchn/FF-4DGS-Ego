sequences with our boxes injected, 0 sequence failures. On its \emph{own} tuned detector it scores
49.7 / 34.7 / 42.3; on our (noisier) boxes it degrades to 87.7 / 40.1 / 53.4. This is the fair,
input-matched number: HaWoR's motion+infiller is tuned to its own box statistics, so under a common
realistic detector our jitter-robust model holds up while HaWoR does not.
$^{c}$ HaPTIC + SLAM: HaPTIC produces no metric depth, so absolute placement is not recoverable
(C-MPJPE 157.7 with a depth-dependent error, while root-relative C-MPJPE is a healthy 29.2 - the pose
is good, only metric placement fails). HaPTIC does no camera tracking, so we compose its per-frame
camera-space hand with the \emph{same} DROID-SLAM trajectory as the other rows (SE(3) residual
0.000\,mm) and score identically. Only WA-MPJPE is meaningful, and it is \tbd\ here because HaPTIC + SLAM has never been composed on our detector boxes: no \texttt{haptic\_slam\_detbox\_preds} exists. Its $35.7$ is a \emph{native}-regime number and is withheld rather than printed in a shared-box column.
$^{e}$ All of our numbers come from a single end-to-end feedforward run (our boxes, no SLAM, predicted
extrinsics, 30-frame WA window); the head is trained jitter-robust, so it tolerates the same noisy
boxes that degrade the offline baselines. Feedforward FPS = 6.59, measured on an NVIDIA GB10 (mean over
30 timed 16-frame clips; a single 30-frame forward pass is 4.5\,fps). Total params 1178.7\,M, trainable
262.4\,M.
$^{f}$ Offline FPS is MEASURED: the full HaWoR pipeline (detect + DROID-SLAM + Metric3D + motion +
infiller) ran 157 sequences of 300-frame video in 5\,h\,18\,min = 2.47 fps, dominated by offline
DROID-SLAM. The load-bearing distinction is \emph{online/feedforward} (single causal pass, no future
frames, no global optimization) vs \emph{offline} (whole-video SLAM), not raw throughput.
$^{g}$ HaMeR$_{\text{mt}}$/HaMeR$_{\text{ft}}$: HaMeR fine-tuned on HOI4D with our absolute-depth
(kp3d\_abs) recipe (mt = metric-tuned translation head; ft = stronger fine-tune), per-frame on our
boxes, composed with the same DROID-SLAM trajectory. Our recipe transfers to a HaMeR backbone
(HaMeR$_{\text{ft}}$+SLAM reaches C-MPJPE 37.4, on par with our feedforward 36.2) - but they remain
\emph{offline} (need whole-video SLAM), whereas ours is a single online pass.
$^{h}$ Dyn-HaMR input-matched: its per-frame estimator seeded from our boxes (WiLoR init) and its
DROID-SLAM stage replaced by our SLAM cameras; PHALP and DROID are bypassed. Dyn-HaMR is an
\emph{optimization} method (per-sequence test-time fitting). Because the input-matched setup omits its
own metric-depth stage, its world-scale is unconstrained and collapses on ego HOI4D: absolute C-MPJPE
and W-MPJPE blow up (1337 / 79\,mm) and are omitted. Only the scale-invariant WA-MPJPE (49.3) is
meaningful. Reported over 156 of 157 sequences.
