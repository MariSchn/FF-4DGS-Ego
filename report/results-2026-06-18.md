# Results & state — 2026-06-18 (H2O metric-hand, the SOTA-comparison number)

One-line: the legitimate cross-subject H2O hand result is in, and on the
Procrustes metric (PA-MPJPE) it is **competitive with H2O SOTA** — pose/shape
quality is there; absolute scale (wrist) is the remaining gap.

## The numbers — fine-tuned head, held-out subject4 (full eval, 1953 clips)
| metric | ours | reference | read |
|---|---|---|---|
| **C-MPJPE** (absolute, cam frame) | **55.0 mm** | HaWoR 51.8 / Hand3R 42.6 (*HOI4D*, not H2O) | close, not matched |
| **PA-MPJPE** (Procrustes) | **41.6 mm** | EgoGrasp H2O ~47 | **better on this metric** |
| RR-MPJPE (root-relative shape) | 49.6 mm | — | — |
| WRIST (absolute root) | 38.9 mm | — | the bottleneck |

Zero-shot baseline (HOT3D head on H2O) was C-MPJPE 254.9 mm → **-78%**.

## AIRTIGHT 21-joint result (update — caveat 1 now CLOSED)
Re-ran the full held-out subject4 eval on **21 joints (16 base + 5 fingertips from the
MANO mesh)** — the standard H2O / EgoGrasp convention:
| metric | 16-joint | **21-joint (airtight)** |
|---|---|---|
| C-MPJPE | 55.0 | **58.0 mm** |
| **PA-MPJPE** | 41.6 | **43.6 mm** ← still **< EgoGrasp ~47** |
| RR-MPJPE | 49.6 | 54.7 mm |
| WRIST | 38.9 | 36.6 mm |

**Fingertip pairing proven numerically:** tips(16:21) RR = 68.8 mm vs base(0:16) RR =
50.2 mm → ratio **1.37×**. A mis-permuted tip set would be >4× (tips are 8-12 cm apart),
so the +4mm PA / +5mm RR going 16→21 is exactly the expected fingertip penalty, not a
remap bug. **We can quote PA-MPJPE 43.6 vs EgoGrasp 47.3 as a real, competitive number.**

## Honest read
- **PA-MPJPE 43.6 (21-joint) < EgoGrasp's ~47** is the headline: the predicted hand
  *articulation* is SOTA-grade on H2O once the global similarity transform is removed.
- **Remaining protocol caveats (minor, worth stating):** hand crop bbox is GT-derived
  (a detector stand-in); we use a 16-frame temporal clip (EgoGrasp may be single-frame —
  but HaWoR/Hand3R are also temporal, so this is in-line). Cross-subject split is the
  H2O standard (train subj1-3, test subj4).
- **Caveat 2 (protocol):** hand crop bbox is derived from GT joints (a detector
  stand-in, standard hand-pose protocol but worth stating).
- **Caveat 3:** C-MPJPE 55.0 is held up by absolute scale (wrist 38.9 mm). The gap
  between C-MPJPE (55) and PA (41.6) **is** the absolute-placement error — exactly
  what the scale-head idea targets.

## What this means for Tier-1
The hand head is competitive on a clean egocentric dataset (H2O). Combined with the
dense-depth metric-scene result (HOI4D 20.5→13.4 cm) and the renderable-Gaussian
novelty, the story holds. The remaining work is (a) make the comparison airtight
(21-joint), (b) close the absolute-scale gap (scale-head / higher res), (c) the
HOI4D/Hand3R comparison (blocked on the manual Hand-Pose download), (d) the figure.
