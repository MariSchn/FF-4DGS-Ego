# Results & state — 2026-06-17 (CVPR dense-depth pivot, day 1)

One-line: pivoted from HOT3D to dense-depth datasets (HOI4D + H2O); the scene-metric
claim works under dense supervision, and the hand-metric (C-MPJPE) pipeline is built
and learning. Not yet competitive with SOTA — that's a scaling question now.

## Results so far
- **Backbone runs on HOI4D** zero-shot: gs_depth finite, ~metric (0.28–1.5 m). Pivot de-risked.
- **Dense-depth (the metric-SCENE differentiator) works:** supervise gs_depth on HOI4D's
  dense sensor depth → depth residual **20.5 cm (frozen) → 13.8 cm** (6 seqs; no masking,
  so Cyrus's degradation worry doesn't appear). Scaled to 18 seqs (running/queued).
- **C-MPJPE (the hand-metric SOTA gate), on H2O:**
  - zero-shot (HOT3D head): **257 mm** (scale doesn't transfer — the honest "before").
  - fine-tuned, within-subject-1 split: **66.6 mm** best (−75%). Converges ~66 mm; wrist
    (~64 mm) is the bottleneck = the up-to-scale-backbone absolute-scale problem.
  - **Caveat:** that 66 mm is within-subject (optimistic). The legitimate **cross-subject**
    number (train subj 1–3, test subj 4) is running now on all 4 packed subjects, with
    8 unfrozen blocks + more epochs.
  - **Positioning caveat:** Hand3R 42.6 / HaWoR 51.8 are **HOI4D** numbers, not H2O — not a
    direct comparison. The apples-to-apples vs Hand3R needs our HOI4D C-MPJPE (next phase).
