## What is DIAGNOSED but NOT fixed

**The scene scale is 42% too small.** Measured against the GT camera scale over 1884 segments:
`s_gt` 1.0230 vs `s_hand` 0.6208, ratio **0.578**. Since the scale multiplies camera *translation*
only, and translation is the dominant W lever (−47.8 mm vs −18.8 mm for rotation), this is a
first-order error on our weakest metric (seg128 W 200.9 against a GT-trajectory oracle of 61.5).

The cause is one-sided: at a hand pixel the nearest visible surface *is* the hand, so any
misregistration blends in **background**, which is strictly farther. `d_scene` can be pushed up and
essentially never down. Hand at 0.5 m against background at 0.85 m gives 1.7 - the size observed.

Two mechanisms are implemented behind flags, **both off by default**, and a 2×2 is queued
(job 104353). Adopt only if W-MPJPE goes down AND the GT ratio moves toward 1.0 AND camera-frame
metrics do **not** move. If nothing moves W, the scale is not the binding term and the camera head
is next - that outcome gets recorded, not buried.

## What is now PROVABLE that was not

Three box-source defects in one day shared a root cause: **box source and trajectory source lived
only in directory names.** `scripts/pred_provenance.py` now writes `_provenance.json` next to
predictions, with `trajectory_source` as a *closed set* so a scorer can test for the GT-oracle case
rather than read prose. The scorer copies it into its output and shouts when it is missing.

This matters because the oracle directories **score better**: `haptic_detbox_preds` reports WA 23.3
against the honest 36.7, and one had already been wired into a seg100 table under a "+SLAM" label.
